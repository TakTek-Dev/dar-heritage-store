Ordering and payment

From the cart to the confirmation message.

How do I order a piece?

Add the piece to your cart from its page, then complete your order on the checkout page. If you prefer, send your cart as it is to DAR on WhatsApp from the cart page.

Do I need an account to order?

No, you can order as a guest with your mobile number. An account keeps your orders, your addresses and the pieces you loved, and you sign in with a code sent to you on WhatsApp from the sign-in page.

Which payment methods can I use?

A bank card through a secure payment page, and your card details never pass through DAR. Or a bank transfer: we send you the details on WhatsApp and prepare your order once the transfer arrives.

Which currency are prices shown in?

Turkish lira. If you pay with a card from outside Turkey, your bank converts the amount into your currency.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, before it ships. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number, and we will change it, or cancel it and refund you.

How do I know where my order is?

On the order tracking page, with your order number and mobile number. When we ship your order, we send you the tracking number on WhatsApp.

Shipping and delivery

Within Turkey, abroad, and from the centre's door.

Where do you ship to?

To every city in Turkey, and abroad with international shipping. You can also collect your order from the centre in Istanbul.

How much is shipping?

Within Turkey, a flat fee shown on the checkout page before you confirm. International shipping is priced by country and weight, and we confirm it with you before you pay. Pickup from the centre is free.

When will my order arrive?

We hand pieces in stock to the courier within a number of days set by DAR of confirming your order. For pieces embroidered to order, we agree a date with you.

Can you ship a gift to someone else?

Yes. Enter their address on the checkout page, choose traditional gift wrapping in the cart and write your message, and DAR will handwrite it on a card.

Can I collect my order from the centre?

Yes. Choose pickup from the centre on the checkout page, and we will tell you on WhatsApp when your order is ready.

Returns and exchanges

14 days to decide. Pieces made for you alone cannot be returned.

Can I return a piece?

Yes. You can return a piece within 14 days of receiving it without giving a reason, as long as it is unused and in its original condition and packaging.

Which pieces cannot be returned?

Pieces tailored to your size or embroidered to your order, and gifts printed with a logo or a name, because they were made for you alone. Earrings too, once their packaging is opened, for hygiene reasons.

How do I return a piece?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and the piece you want to return, and we will send you how to post it.

Who pays for return shipping?

Set by DAR. If your piece arrives faulty or different from what you ordered, the shipping is on us.

When do I get my money back?

Within 14 days of your return request reaching us, by the same method you paid with.

Can I exchange for another size or colour?

Yes, if it is available, under the same conditions as returns.

Caring for your pieces

Hand embroidery lasts a lifetime if you treat it gently.

Wash by handIn cold water with a gentle detergent, and do not leave it soaking for long.
Do not wring itPress it between two towels to draw the water out. Wringing pulls the stitches.
Dry it in the shadeLaid flat on a level surface, away from direct sun so the colours do not fade.
Iron it from the backOn the reverse side of the embroidery, with a cotton cloth in between.
Store it foldedIn a fabric bag, away from damp. Do not leave it hanging for long, or it will stretch.
Frames and accessoriesHang frames away from the sun, and wipe accessories with a dry cloth, away from perfume.

Special orders

A piece just for you, or gifts in your organisation's name.

Do you embroider pieces to order?

Send us your idea, your size and your colours, and we will tell you whether it is possible and how long it will take.

Do you offer gifts for organisations?

Yes: plaques, frames and boxes with your organisation's logo and formal wrapping. Ask for a quote on the corporate gifts page.

Can I add a message to a gift?

Yes. In the cart, choose traditional gift wrapping and write your message, and DAR will handwrite it on a card.
