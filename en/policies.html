The pieces

We show every piece with its photos, description and size. Because the embroidery is done by hand, a piece can differ slightly from its photo in shade and in the detail of the stitches. This is part of its nature, not a fault.

Prices

Prices are in Turkish lira and include tax to be confirmed by DAR. The shipping cost appears on the checkout page before you confirm your order.

Order confirmation

Your order is confirmed when you receive the confirmation message with your order number. If a piece sells out after you order it, we will tell you and refund you in full.

Payment

By bank card through a licensed payment provider, or by bank transfer. DAR does not store card details and never sees them.

Right of withdrawal

You may withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving the piece without giving a reason, under Turkey's distance contracts regulation, with the exceptions listed in Returns and exchanges.

Disputes

We always try to solve any problem with you directly. You also keep the right to apply to the consumer arbitration committees or consumer courts in Turkey, within the limits set by law.

Shipping and delivery

Where we ship

To every city in Turkey, and abroad with international shipping. Orders can also be collected from the centre in Istanbul at no cost.

Timing

We hand your order to the courier within a number of days set by DAR of confirming it. Pieces embroidered to order have a date we agree with you before we begin.

Cost

  • Within Turkey: a flat fee shown on the checkout page or free above an amount set by DAR.
  • Outside Turkey: by country and weight, confirmed with you before you pay. Customs duties in the destination country are paid by the recipient.
  • Pickup from the centre: free.

Tracking and receiving

We send you the tracking number on WhatsApp, and you can follow your order on the tracking page. Check the parcel in front of the courier. If it is damaged, ask for a written report, do not accept it, and then let us know.

Returns and exchanges

Period and conditions

Within 14 days of receiving it, and the piece must be unused, in its original condition, with its tag and packaging.

What cannot be returned

  • Pieces tailored to your size or embroidered to your order.
  • Gifts printed with a logo or a name.
  • Earrings, once their packaging is opened, for hygiene reasons.

Steps

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and the piece.
  2. We send you how to ship it to the centre.
  3. We check the piece when it arrives.
  4. We refund you within 14 days of your return request reaching us, by the same payment method.

Return shipping cost

Set by DAR. If your piece arrives faulty or different from what you ordered, we replace it or refund you, and in both cases the shipping is on us.

Privacy and data protection

What we collect

Your name, your mobile number, your email if you add it, your delivery address and the details of your order.

Why

To prepare and deliver your order and to contact you about it, and to keep the records the law requires.

Who we share it with

With the courier, as far as delivery needs, and with the payment provider to complete payment. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as the law requires, then we delete it.

Your rights

Under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, you have the right to know what we keep about you, and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Message us on WhatsApp or at an email set by DAR, and we will reply within the period set by law.

What the site keeps in your browser

The site keeps your cart and your favourite pieces in your own browser, so they are still there when you come back. We do not use advertising trackers, and you can clear what was saved from your browser settings at any time.

A question about your order?

Write to us with your order number, and we will answer you ourselves.
